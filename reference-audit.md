# Lunaria reference audit

Reference: https://dribbble.com/shots/26594325-Lunaria-Web-Design-for-Premium-Beauty-Studio by Phenomenon Labs / Phenomenon Studio. Inspected in the browser and all five available static design boards downloaded to `reference/` for comparison only. These boards are not website assets.

## Observed composition

- Full-bleed beauty portrait hero, compact white overlaid navigation, brown rectangular booking button, left-aligned supporting sentence and underlined booking link, enormous white BEAUTY SALON title across the bottom. Mobile title wraps to two lines.
- Generously spaced white introduction with centered, tightly tracked uppercase typography. Small four-corner bracket labels interrupt the headline. A narrow centered explanatory paragraph and underlined link finish it.
- Service overview: three photographic rectangular category tiles on a pale neutral field. Detailed services view: large centered heading, horizontal outlined category cards, two-column category introduction, fine-bordered four-column service grid punctuated by large treatment photos and small bracket motifs. Cards include prices, time, explanatory copy and direct booking.
- The overview shows a bold typographic results section, review cards, and a brown photographic story panel. Fabricated reviews, ratings, before/after images and metrics will be replaced with honest experience content.
- Gallery: asymmetric, staggered rectangular interior photographs distributed around an oversized OUR SPACE heading. Captions underneath; tiny expansion marks. Mobile uses compact staggered columns and a clear booking CTA.
- Remaining boards show a warm photographic consultation banner, three-column editorial/newsletter imagery, compact multi-column footer and very large closing BEAUTY SALON typography. The author's accompanying description mentions simple team portraits and a minimal contact form. Their precise full layouts are not legible in the five supplied static boards; implement restrained matching layouts without claiming exact reconstruction.

## Visual system

White/off-white #FDFDFC, near-black #111718, warm brown #534438; terracotta #B76442 and warm neutral #B08F6F sparingly. Teal/cream gradients surround the presentation boards and are NOT page backgrounds. Use a bold neo-grotesque, tight tracking (approximately -0.06em headings), uppercase scale roughly 72–110px on desktop, very large viewport-fitted hero type, small utilitarian body text. Square corners, 1px borders, underlined text links, almost no shadows, expansive whitespace. Approximately 40–64px desktop gutters and 20px mobile gutters.

## BEAUTI BOX adaptation

Preserve supplied orange logo artwork intact (no replacement symbol or speculative tracing). Use a matching plain text wordmark where small-size legibility requires it, accompanied by the original artwork. Prominently include supplied actual studio photograph in gallery. Clearly identify all other stock photographs as illustrative and staff placeholders as demo content. Keep orange restrained. Centralize unconfirmed contact fields and sample INR service prices.

## Limits of static concepts

Exact font family, full responsive specifications, timing/easing, real menu behavior, booking backend, full card detail pages, contact destinations and business information cannot be determined. Responsive breakpoints and interactions will be implemented independently and tested. No Dribbble photo or design board will ship inside public assets.
